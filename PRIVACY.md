# Privacy

football-engine is a personal automation run by its owner for their own use.

- **What it reads:** the owner's own YouTube data, accessed with the owner's own authorization, plus public YouTube video and channel statistics (titles, view counts, durations, subscriber counts) used for content research.
- **What it reads and writes:** only the owner's own Google Drive files and Google Sheets, and only for this automation.
- **What it stores:** nothing about YouTube viewers or other users. Public video and channel statistics used for research are kept only in the owner's own Google Drive and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
- **What it shares:** nothing. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.

Credentials and API keys are kept out of this repository (see [README.md](README.md#secrets)).
